# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NexDownload is a technical project for people with different backgrounds and experience
levels. Participants are expected to make the project a respectful place to report bugs,
ask questions, review code, and propose changes.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good faith.
- Respect privacy: redact tokens, credentials, personal paths, and private URLs.
- Credit prior work and respect licenses and trademarks.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution for scope, security, maintenance,
  or originality reasons.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing another person’s private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting copied proprietary code, assets, or documentation.
- Spam, malicious links, or attempts to compromise the project or its users.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a problem in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, chats, and
other project spaces, and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the repository’s private
contact method or a GitHub security advisory. Do not open a public issue containing
personal or sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed as promptly as practical, kept
confidential to the extent possible, and handled with proportionate action.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when this code is violated.
